03

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, CoGG Safe may process the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Username or account identifier
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address, where provided
  • Account authentication information
  • User role and account-related information

Emergency and SOS Information

  • SOS event information
  • Emergency status and timestamps
  • Emergency recipient information
  • Location information associated with an SOS event
  • Images and short audio recordings associated with an SOS event

Location Information

When location-related features are enabled and permission is granted, CoGG Safe may process your device location to provide emergency and live-location functionality.

Live Location Limit: Live location sharing automatically stops after a maximum period of 3 hours.
04

How We Use Information

Information processed by CoGG Safe may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing authorized user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing SOS and emergency assistance functionality
  • Sharing emergency information with authorized recipients
  • Providing live-location functionality when enabled
  • Sending emergency-related communications
  • Storing and synchronizing SOS information
  • Maintaining and operating the application
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the service
  • Investigating technical problems and service issues

Information is not intended to be collected for unrelated purposes outside the functionality and operation of CoGG Safe.

05

SOS and Emergency Data

CoGG Safe is designed to provide emergency assistance functionality. When an SOS event is initiated, the application may process information required to support the emergency workflow.

Depending on the features enabled and available connectivity, this may include location information, emergency contact information, images, short audio recordings, timestamps, and other information associated with the SOS event.

Emergency media: SOS-related media may be stored using Cloudflare R2 as part of the application's emergency data infrastructure.
06

Live Location

CoGG Safe may provide live-location functionality for emergency and safety purposes when the required permissions have been granted.

Live location is limited to a maximum period of 3 hours. After this period, live-location sharing automatically stops.

Location information may also be associated with an SOS event where required to support emergency functionality.
