2. How we use your information

  • To operate the core safety feature: create and dispatch SOS events, and notify your group's administrators and members
  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To generate the public, time-limited emergency link that lets a responder view an active SOS without installing the app
  • To send push notifications and email alerts related to SOS events
  • To maintain SOS and notification history inside the app
  • To monitor, secure and troubleshoot the Service (e.g. rate-limiting abusive logins)
  • To comply with legal obligations, where applicable

We do not use your SOS media, location or message content for advertising, and we do not sell this data.

3. What happens during an SOS

Triggering an SOS is a deliberate sharing action. When it happens:

  • Your group's administrators and other active members of your group are notified (push notification and/or email).
  • Your live location, captured photos and audio become visible to your group's administrators from the admin dashboard, and to anyone holding the unique, randomly-generated emergency link while the SOS remains active.
  • The emergency link works without a login, so treat it like any other sensitive link — it is only intended to be shared with people who should be able to see your SOS (e.g. forwarded to a nearby responder).
  • Live location sharing automatically stops after [MAX_LIVE_LOCATION_HOURS] hours, or sooner if you or an administrator ends the SOS.
  • SMS and phone calls made as part of an SOS are sent directly from your device using your own SIM/network — this content passes through your mobile carrier as it would for any SMS/call you send yourself.

7. Data security

  • Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text).
  • Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to the admin dashboard and SOS data is restricted by authentication and role checks.
  • Login attempts are rate-limited to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.
  • The public emergency link uses a long, random, single-purpose token and only remains valid while the SOS is active.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.
